How our service works.

We’re here to make designing your space feel clear and personal.

Send us an enquiry through the form below, on WhatsApp or by phone, and it goes straight to our founders. We talk through your space, priorities and budget, then prepare a design direction and a free estimate. Once you approve, we handle design, vendors, execution and timelines under one roof, and our support continues after handover.

Just your name, number and what you are looking for. If you have a floor plan, photos of the space or reference images, share them on WhatsApp and we can give you a clearer estimate.

Every project is priced on its scope, so we start with a free estimate. We use premium materials without inflated agency pricing, and our clients often mention our transparent pricing.

It depends on the size and scope, and we share a clear timeline with your estimate. As a reference, our corporate projects of 12,000 to 20,000 sq.ft were delivered in 60 to 90 days.
